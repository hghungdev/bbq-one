Privacy Policy — BBQOne

Last updated: April 2025

BBQOne is a Chrome extension for personal note-taking, bookmark management, and dictionary lookup. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to provide the service:

  • Authentication credentials — email and password, used solely to log in via Supabase Auth.
  • Notes and folders — content you create, stored in your personal Supabase account.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal identity information (name, address, phone number).
  • We do not collect browsing history or track visited pages.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not collect payment or financial information.

How Data Is Used

Your data is used exclusively to provide the core features of BBQOne. It is never sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.

Data Storage & Security

Notes are stored in your personal Supabase project, protected by Row Level Security (RLS) — only you can access your own data. Notes inside Secure Folders are encrypted client-side (AES-GCM) before being sent to the server.

Third-Party Services

BBQOne connects to Supabase (supabase.co) for authentication and data sync.

Translation Service Disclosure

When you use the translation feature, BBQOne uses two providers:

  1. Chrome Built-in Translator API (local, offline). Translation is performed entirely on your device. No data leaves your browser.
  2. MyMemory Translation API (higher quality, network-dependent). When available, BBQOne sends the selected text via HTTPS to MyMemory for translation. We do NOT send any personal information, user IDs, IP tracking codes, or browsing context — only the selected text. MyMemory's privacy policy: https://mymemory.translated.net/doc/privacy.php

You can disable MyMemory in Settings → Translation Quality to use only the Chrome built-in translator (100% local, no network required).
